# Malten

Malten is the North American Business Ecosystem project within this repository.

## Purpose

Malten is being developed as a separate application area from SurplusClaim USA so its dashboard, training environment, business modules, and eventual production deployment can evolve independently.

## Current product areas

- Malten Command Center
- Leads & CRM
- AI Automations
- Opportunity Exchange
- Malten Business Passport
- Malten AI
- Malten Leads
- Malten Supply
- Malten Trades
- Malten Secure
- Malten Logistics
- Training Sandbox

## Environment separation

The Training Sandbox must remain isolated from production. Training records, simulated subscriptions, sample customers, practice opportunities, and automation exercises must never trigger live outreach, real charges, or production integrations.

## Deployment direction

The Malten application will be prepared for an HTTPS beta deployment from this `/malten` project area. Production integrations remain disabled until authentication, database isolation, permissions, billing, API credentials, and security controls are configured and tested.

## Status

Malten dashboard prototype and training workflow are under active development.
