# Claimant talking-point examples

Opening: identify SurplusClaim USA as a private service, state that a public record may show surplus funds, explain that the claimant may be able to claim directly, and ask permission to explain.

“I think this is a scam”: encourage independent verification; provide the government office/source reference; do not request sensitive information merely to prove legitimacy.

“I can do this myself”: agree. Explain that the paid service is optional assistance with research, entitlement, documents, deadlines and workflow.

“Your fee is too high”: quote only from a VERIFIED rule. Explain the fee and direct-claim option without pressure.

“Not interested”: ask once whether they want the public-record reference or no further contact. If they decline contact, suppress.

“Call me later”: record the requested callback and do not contact earlier.

“Stop calling”: acknowledge and immediately activate do-not-contact across channels.
