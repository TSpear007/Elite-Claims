# Claimant data privacy

Use public records only for lawful business purposes and minimize personal data. Do not expose claimant contact information in public pages. Restrict case/contact data by role, encrypt sensitive data in transit/at rest, audit access, and establish retention/deletion policies. Do not request SSN, bank information or identity documents during initial outreach. Use vetted providers and secure claimant workflows when identity verification or payment data is actually required.
