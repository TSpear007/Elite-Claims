# AI Claims Engine

The score is an explainable triage aid from 0–100. Signals include official-source verification, claimant identity confidence, lien-risk estimate, surplus size, deadline urgency and whether the applicable rule is verified.

Outputs: score, priority, reasons and risk flags. A high score moves a case to human entitlement/lien review; it never authorizes outreach, determines ownership, promises recovery or substitutes for legal analysis.

Hard gates operate separately from the score: jurisdiction verification, waiting periods, channel restrictions and do-not-contact suppression.
