# Law library fields

Each state/county/surplus-type record stores: controlling statute/ordinance citation, official source URL, fee model, max fee percentage, max fee dollars, waiting period, solicitation restrictions, licensing requirements, agreement/disclosure requirements, filing notes, self-claim disclosure, effective date, reviewer and last verification date.

County procedure and state law are separate concepts. The county may supply forms, deadlines and process details while state law controls fees/licensing/assignments. Federal requirements can also apply. The platform therefore never promises that one county rule is complete merely because a county webpage was imported.
