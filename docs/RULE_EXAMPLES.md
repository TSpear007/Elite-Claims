# Official-law configuration examples (review before activation)

These examples demonstrate why fees cannot be hard-coded nationally.

Texas Tax Code §34.04: official statute text provides that a non-attorney may not charge an owner a fee to obtain covered tax-sale excess proceeds; attorney fees have a statutory cap. Configure as restricted/non-attorney prohibited after current legal review.

Arizona A.R.S. §33-812(P): covered third-party recovery agreements must be written/acknowledged, agreements made before the statutory 30-day period are void, and fees above $2,500 face a statutory reasonableness rule. Configure waiting period + dollar rule after review.

Nevada's 2025 enacted excess-proceeds provisions include a 10% limit for covered recovery arrangements. Confirm the currently codified section/applicability before marking VERIFIED.

Florida tax-deed surplus under §197.582 and mortgage-foreclosure surplus under §§45.032-.033 are separate workflows. Never copy an unclaimed-property Chapter 717 fee rule into those workflows without legal basis.
