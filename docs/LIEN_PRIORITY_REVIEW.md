# Lien/entitlement review

Before promising service value or filing: verify former ownership and sale record; identify subordinate mortgages, tax liens, HOA/condo liens, judgments and other recorded claims; check whether lienholders filed timely claims; identify death/estate/heir issues; verify assignments; confirm the holding agency and current balance; confirm deadline/procedure; escalate disputed priority or legal interpretation to qualified counsel.

AI risk flags prioritize this work; they do not decide legal priority.
