# Rejection pivot rules

Allowed: answer a concern, provide independent verification, explain optional service value, offer a requested callback, or send the public source when permitted.

Not allowed: repeated persuasion after a clear no; false urgency; implying government affiliation; hiding the self-claim option; changing the fee to evade a cap; promising recovery; pressuring elderly/vulnerable claimants; or contacting after a do-not-contact request.

A plain “not interested” permits one clarification: public-source reference or no further contact. A stop/do-not-contact statement permits no sales pivot and immediately suppresses the case.
