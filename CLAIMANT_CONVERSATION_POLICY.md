# Claimant conversation policy

A pivot is a clarification, not pressure. Agents may answer a concern once with transparent facts and an independent-verification option. They must not manufacture urgency, imply government affiliation, conceal self-claim options, promise entitlement, or repeatedly pursue a claimant who asks to stop.

## Outcomes
- NEED_PROOF: provide official agency/source reference; request no sensitive data.
- SCAM_CONCERN: encourage independent verification and identify SurplusClaim USA as private.
- SELF_CLAIM: acknowledge the option and explain only the optional service value.
- FEE_CONCERN: quote only from a VERIFIED rule and disclose the direct-claim option.
- NOT_INTERESTED: ask once whether they want the public record reference or no further contact.
- CALLBACK: record the requested time; no earlier contact.
- DO_NOT_CONTACT: suppress immediately across channels.

No script may override the compliance engine, waiting period, channel restriction or suppression list.
