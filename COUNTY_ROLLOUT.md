# Nationwide county rollout

The platform supports every U.S. county/jurisdiction structurally, but legal/fee records are activated one jurisdiction and surplus type at a time.

For each county: (1) register official source; (2) upload/pull list; (3) identify controlling state statute plus county procedure; (4) record source citations/effective date; (5) verify fee cap, waiting period, licensing, solicitation and agreement requirements; (6) compliance/counsel changes rule to VERIFIED; (7) fee/contact engines become available for that rule; (8) reverify periodically and whenever law/procedure changes.

Never copy a rule from a neighboring county/state merely because the process looks similar. State law may control while county procedure supplies forms/deadlines; both must be captured.
